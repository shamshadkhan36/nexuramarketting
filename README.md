# AGENCEE — Digital Marketing & Creative Agency Website

Reconstructed to match the **AGENCEE** Framer agency aesthetic with dark obsidian backgrounds, arched ribbed textures, vivid flame orange accents (`#ff7a00`), floating capsule navigation, circular statistics badges, team showcase with background marquee text, and rotating circular video player badges.

---

- Right CTA: Bright Orange Pill Button `TEMPLATE >` with black chevron.

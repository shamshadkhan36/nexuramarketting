# AGENCEE — Digital Marketing & Creative Agency Website (Black & Blue Gradient Theme)

Reconstructed to match the **AGENCEE** Framer agency aesthetic with **Obsidian Black and Electric Blue Gradients** (`#05070b`, `#0ea5e9`, `#06b6d4`, `#2563eb`), arched ribbed textures, floating capsule navigation, circular statistics badges, team showcase with background marquee text, and rotating circular video player badges.

---

## 🌟 Features & Color Palette

### 🎨 **Color Scheme: Black & Electric Blue Gradient**
- **Deep Obsidian Black Backgrounds**: `#05070b` / `#0a0d14`
- **Midnight Card Backgrounds**: `#0e121a` / `#131824`
- **Gradient Accents**: Electric Blue to Neon Cyan (`linear-gradient(135deg, #0ea5e9 0%, #2563eb 50%, #4f46e5 100%)`)
- **Cyan Glows & Highlights**: `#06b6d4`, `#38bdf8`
- **Border Accents**: Soft translucent white borders with cyan hover transitions (`rgba(14, 165, 233, 0.4)`)

---
